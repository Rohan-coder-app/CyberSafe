A good rule of thumb

If a message makes you panic, pay, or act right now, take a breath and check it another way.

Go to the company’s website yourself instead of using the message link.

WHEN SOMETHING FEELS OFF

Take a moment. Check these three things.

You don’t have to be a tech expert. Start with what you can see.

  1. 01

    Who sent it?

    Look closely at the sender’s address, not just the name.

  2. 02

    Are they rushing you?

    Unexpected pressure to pay, sign in, or share details is a reason to slow down.

  3. 03

    Can you check another way?

    Contact the person or company using a number or website you already trust.
